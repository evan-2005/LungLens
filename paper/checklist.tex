\section*{NeurIPS paper checklist}

\begin{enumerate}

\item \textbf{Claims.} Do the abstract and introduction state claims that match the paper's contributions and scope?\\
\answerYes{The central claim, that the deployed summary does not read the mask, is a source-level fact quoted verbatim in Section~\ref{sec:reportgen} and Appendix~\ref{app:templates}. The abstract and Section~\ref{sec:limitations} state which evaluation-grid cells are measured and which are proposed.}

\item \textbf{Limitations.} Does the paper discuss the limitations of the work?\\
\answerYes{Section~\ref{sec:limitations}: unrun cells (H2, H4, S3/S4), no clinician ratings, dataset-source confounding, label noise in the dominant error mode, and the reproducibility fragility of the split.}

\item \textbf{Theory.} Are assumptions and proofs given for every theoretical result?\\
\answerNA{The paper reports measurements, not theorems. The one derived bound (localisation quality upper-bounds spatial-claim correctness) is stated with the Dice measurement it rests on.}

\item \textbf{Experimental reproducibility.} Is there enough information to reproduce the main results?\\
\answerYes{The audited system is public (repository withheld for double blind). The descriptor extractor, the summary reconstructions, the thresholds, and the derangement seed are in Section~\ref{sec:design}; the 245-film scoring table and mask arrays are the system's own published analysis outputs.}

\item \textbf{Open access to data and code.} Are data and code available?\\
\answerYes{The audited system and its analysis outputs are public. No new model was trained.}

\item \textbf{Experimental settings.} Are training and test details specified?\\
\answerYes{For the components this paper adds there is no training (Section~\ref{sec:design}). The fixed upstream classifier and U-Net settings are in Section~\ref{sec:system} and Appendix~\ref{app:classification}.}

\item \textbf{Error bars.} Are error bars or other measures of statistical significance reported?\\
\answerYes{Distributions (latency, Dice) carry a mean and standard deviation or a median. Single-number rates are exact counts over the full 245-film pool, so a confidence interval would describe sampling that did not occur.}

\item \textbf{Compute.} Is the compute for the experiments reported?\\
\answerYes{The analysis is geometry over saved mask arrays and runs in seconds on a CPU; latency figures are from one laptop CPU (i7-13620H, 10 threads). No GPU, no training.}

\item \textbf{Code of ethics.} Does the research conform to the NeurIPS Code of Ethics?\\
\answerYes{A retrospective audit of an existing research and educational prototype; no patient contact; the interface already carries a non-diagnostic disclaimer this paper argues should be more prominent.}

\item \textbf{Broader impacts.} Are potential positive and negative societal impacts discussed?\\
\answerYes{Section~\ref{sec:limitations} (Broader impact): the persuasiveness of a label plus a plausible heatmap plus a fluent summary, and why this paper is a caution against it.}

\item \textbf{Safeguards.} Are safeguards described for any high-risk release?\\
\answerNA{No models, datasets, or scrapers are released.}

\item \textbf{Licenses.} Are existing assets credited and their licenses respected?\\
\answerYes{Backbone, localisation method, and segmentation architecture are cited in Section~\ref{sec:system}. The eight source datasets are public Kaggle datasets, named by their exact identifiers so each license can be consulted on its own page:
\begin{itemize}\setlength{\itemsep}{0pt}\ttfamily\footnotesize

\item raddar/tuberculosis-chest-xrays-montgomery

\item legrande/tbdata
\end{itemize}
Terms differ per dataset; the camera-ready should transcribe each from its source page.}

\item \textbf{New assets.} Are new assets documented?\\
\answerNA{No new assets are released with this submission.}

\item \textbf{Crowdsourcing and human subjects.} Are crowdsourcing and IRB details given?\\
\answerNA{No human subjects, no crowdsourcing, no clinician annotation was collected.}

\item \textbf{IRB / risk for human subjects.} Were risks disclosed and approvals obtained?\\
\answerNA{No human-subjects research was conducted.}

\item \textbf{LLM usage.} Is any LLM use in the core method declared?\\
\answerNA{No LLM is used in the method. S2/S3/S4 LLM conditions are named as out of scope and not run.}

\end{enumerate}
